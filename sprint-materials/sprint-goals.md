# Sprint Goals
*Instructions: For each sprint, write a one or two sentence goal plus the single
demoable outcome that proves the sprint succeeded. Keep in mind that you will
conduct user studies after sprints 1 and 2 to get feedback to inform future
development, so define MVP goals to be most helpful for that. The sprint 3 MVP
should be your final product.*

## Sprint 1
**Goal:** Stand up a publicly hosted, WCAG 2.2 AA-compliant web application connected to our existing Project 2 PostgreSQL database (AWS) through the Java/JDBC backend, with Google OAuth2 account creation/login and role-based access. Deliver the first workflows for all three user types so User Study 1 can test them: customer order history and menu photos, manager restock and profit-margin reports, and cashier schedule viewing and editable bowls/plates.
**Outcome:** Deployed URL where a customer can create an account, log in, view their past month's orders, and see a photo for every menu item; a manager can view restock and profit-margin reports; and a cashier can view their schedule and add/remove items in bowls/plates while taking an order.

## Sprint 2
**Goal:** Expand to authenticated customer self-ordering during service hours and deliver a first working version of our novel feature, [NOVEL FEATURE NAME], so User Study 2 can gather real customer feedback. *(Teams of 5: kitchen staff can view live inventory.)*
**Outcome:** Deployed URL where a customer can log in and place their own order while the novel feature works end-to-end for at least one user type, with User Study 1 improvements folded in.

## Sprint 3
**Goal:** Ship the final product: refine the novel feature and all interfaces based on User Study 2 feedback, complete the manager trend-analysis and reporting suite, and harden accessibility, performance, and stability.
**Outcome:** Final deployed MVP with complete, polished workflows for every user type (cashier, manager, customer, [kitchen staff if 5-person team]), the fully realized novel feature, and verified WCAG 2.2 AA compliance *(Honors: translation on all interfaces and a responsive mobile customer experience)*.
